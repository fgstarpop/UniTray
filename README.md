## 📖 What You Need

Before installing UniTray, ensure your system meets these requirements:

- Windows 10 or higher
- PowerShell version 5.1 or higher
- Administrative privileges for installation

## 🔧 Features

- **Monitor Packages:** Keep track of your installed packages and their updates.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** A simple tray application that integrates seamlessly with your Windows environment.
- **Automatic Updates:** Receive updates directly through the app for a hassle-free experience.
- **Multi-Platform Support:** Works with Scoop, Chocolatey, and WinGet packages.

## 📊 Using UniTray

Once you launch UniTray, you’ll see a tray icon on your taskbar. Here’s how to navigate its features:

- **Monitor Packages:** Click on the icon to open the main interface where you can view all installed packages.
- **Check for Updates:** Use the update button to scan for any available updates for your packages.
- **Settings:** Access the settings to customize how UniTray works and interacts with your packages.
